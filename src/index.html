Cat Paw Air Hockey

Place the phone flat in portrait orientation with one player at each short end. Each player touches and holds the ready paw on their own half. After the countdown, drag one finger to move your paw striker. Score in the opposite cat goal. First to five wins. Both players hold their ready paws again for a rematch.
