# CATPAW-EXPANSION-001

## Scope

Fullscreen, a mirrored paused settings menu, eight bounded handicap controls, final-score PNG capture, and local PNG themes extend the settled shared-phone game. The expansion preserves SFHS Pixi v8 ownership, one visible canvas, fixed-step rules, local-only operation, and the baseline first-to-five flow.

## Settings contract

All settings use 5% steps and default to 100%. Puck and individual paw speed range from 70% to 130%; puck, individual paw, and individual defensive goal sizes range from 75% to 125%. Changes made during motion become pending and apply only at a goal-reset, next serve, or rematch boundary.
